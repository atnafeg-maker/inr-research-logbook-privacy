Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

INR & Vitamin K Logbook is designed as an offline-first personal health logbook. We respect your privacy and do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal or health information on external servers.

1. Data Storage

All INR values, medication entries, dietary logs, and calibration settings are stored locally on your device using internal application storage. Deleting the app or clearing app storage permanently removes your data.

2. Third-Party Sharing

No data is shared with third parties, advertisers, or analytics networks. Any CSV export you initiate is controlled entirely by you through your device's native share menu.

3. Medical Disclaimer

This app is for educational and personal record-keeping purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
